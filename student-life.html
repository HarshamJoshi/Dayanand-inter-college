Student Life

Beyond the classroom: clubs, sports, and events.

Co-Curricular Activities

Sports & Athletics

We believe in physical fitness and teamwork. Our students regularly participate in and win district-level tournaments in Cricket, Volleyball, and Athletics.

Cultural Society

Celebrating our rich heritage through dance, music, and drama. The cultural society organizes the Annual Day function and various inter-house competitions.

Science & Eco Club

Promoting environmental awareness and scientific temper. Students participate in tree plantation drives, science exhibitions, and cleanliness campaigns.
