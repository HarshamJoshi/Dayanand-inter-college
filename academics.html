Academic Programs

Comprehensive state-board aligned curriculum.

Primary School (1-5)

Focus on foundational literacy, numeracy, and environmental awareness through engaging and interactive methods.

  • English & Hindi Languages
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies
  • Art & Craft

Middle School (6-8)

Broadening the academic horizon with structured subjects to prepare students for secondary education.

  • Science (Physics, Chemistry, Bio basics)
  • Mathematics
  • Social Sciences
  • Computer Science

Secondary School (9-10)

Rigorous preparation for board examinations with in-depth subject knowledge and practical applications.

  • Science (Theory & Practicals)
  • Advanced Mathematics
  • Social Science
  • Information Technology

Senior Secondary (11-12)

Specialized streams (Science, Commerce, Arts) guiding students towards higher education and career paths.

  • Science: PCM / PCB
  • Commerce: Accounts, Business Studies
  • Arts: History, Geography, Pol. Science
