About Our School

Our History & Inspiration

Established in 1987 in the serene foothills of Takana, Pithoragarh, Dayanand Inter College draws its name and spirit from the great Indian social reformer and founder of the Arya Samaj, Swami Dayanand Saraswati. Just as Swami Dayanand championed education, rationalism, and equal opportunity for all, our institution carries forward that legacy — providing quality education to every child, regardless of background. Over the decades, we have grown into a premier center of learning in Uttarakhand.

Our Mission

To foster academic excellence, character building, and social responsibility in our students. Inspired by the ideals of Swami Dayanand Saraswati, we strive to create a dynamic learning environment that encourages critical thinking, creativity, and a lifelong love for learning — preparing our students to be responsible and enlightened citizens.

Board Affiliation

Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE)

We are a recognized institution affiliated with the Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE), offering a rigorous and comprehensive curriculum from Class 6 up to the Intermediate (Class 12) level across Science, Commerce, and Arts streams.
