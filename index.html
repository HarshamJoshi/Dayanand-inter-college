About Our School

Dayanand Inter College, located in Takana, Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, has been a beacon of academic excellence since 1987. Under the guidance of our Principal, Mr. Basant Upreti, and Manager, Mr. Ganga Datt Joshi, we are committed to providing a holistic education that nurtures the intellectual, physical, and moral development of our students.
