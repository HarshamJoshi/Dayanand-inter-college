Admissions Process

Join the Dayanand Inter College family.

How to Apply

1

Submit Inquiry / Registration Form

Fill out the online inquiry form on this page or collect the physical form from the school office.

2

Document Submission

Submit required documents including previous year's mark sheet, transfer certificate, birth certificate, and photographs.

3

Interaction / Entrance Test

Students applying for higher grades will have a brief interaction with the Principal and a basic competency test.

4

Fee Payment

Upon selection, pay the admission fee to secure the seat.
